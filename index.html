GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked
questions.

Pricing, scheduling, payments, and rescheduling—before you book.

What are the session prices?

A standard mock interview is ₹129, an HR interview is ₹99, 1:1 guidance is ₹49, and a same-day mock interview request is ₹179.

How does same-day scheduling work?

Select the same-day mock interview option. The form automatically sets your preferred date to today. We will prioritize your application and do our best to schedule your mock interview on the same day.

What happens in a 1:1 guidance session?

It is a personal conversation focused on your career goals, applications, resume or LinkedIn, and preparation strategy. You can discuss your questions and leave with practical next steps tailored to you.

What happens in an HR interview?

The HR interview helps you practise common HR and behavioural questions, explain your background and goals, and receive feedback on how clearly and confidently you communicate. It costs ₹99 per session.

What happens in a mock interview?

We aim to create a professional environment that feels like a real interview. Your interviewer will ask role-relevant questions, discuss your experience and projects, and share constructive feedback to help you prepare for actual interviews.

How long is an interview?

Mock interviews typically last 1 to 1.5 hours.

Will I get my preferred interview format?

Your preferred format is a request. The final format will depend on our availability at that time, and we will confirm it with your schedule.

When is my session confirmed?

Your preferred date is a request. The team will confirm the final time and date after reviewing your application and payment proof.

Can I request a reschedule?

You may request a reschedule by contacting the NextRound team with your name and confirmed session details. Rescheduling is subject to availability.

What should I do after paying?

Submit the application form with your payment screenshot. We will review it and contact you to confirm the session details.

Do I need to upload my resume?

After successfully submitting your application, you can upload your updated resume in the confirmation pop-up.
